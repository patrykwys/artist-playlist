Measure the ranking formula

Pick 5–10 playlists you made and love. For each, three of its artists go in as seeds and the score is how many of its other artists come back in the top 50 (the hit rate). A change is only worth keeping when its verdict is Better: higher on playlists like yours, not just on average on these. With fewer than five playlists every verdict stays "too close to call". The first run reads a lot from Last.fm (several minutes); reruns use the cache.
